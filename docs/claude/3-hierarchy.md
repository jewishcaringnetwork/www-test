# Phase 3: Hierarchy

Working notes for step 3 (hierarchy for the prototype pages). Copy lives in
[./2-content.md](./2-content.md); this doc only records order and priority.

## Audience order (client, 11/16/2026)

1. Donors
2. Volunteers
3. Families

Families come last on the page but must never be more than one click from help:
keep a quiet "Need support?" link in the hero and the support teaser.

## Home: section order (done for the prototype, 11/16/2026)

1. **Hero** (donors): short hero copy from the content draft ("When serious
   illness strikes, the entire family is affected" + blurb), Give now (primary),
   Volunteer (secondary), quiet "Need support?" link
2. **Impact and trust**: 30 years, $ saved, overnight stays
3. **Services teaser**: answers "what do you do?"; three cards + "See all
   services" link (see below)
4. **Donate teaser**: "Your kindness becomes a meal, a night's rest, a moment of
   normal"
5. **Volunteer**: "Lend a hand, a heart, or a few hours"
6. **Support teaser** (families): request support, call
7. **Footer**

Removed: newsletter sign-up (11/16/2026). Not used: the "Longer Hero" three "I
need / I want" cards from the content draft (meant as section 2): they repeat
the support, donate, and volunteer sections and put families on equal footing at
#2. Their only unique job, a door to Tikva House, is covered by the services
teaser.

Each section's single job will be settled while building. Impact numbers are
still an open Phase 2 item: use placeholders marked as such in the prototype.

### Services teaser cards (11/16/2026)

Each card: name, icon, one line. The one line should name specifics, so the
label doesn't have to carry it alone.

- **Health & wellness:** hospital visitation, counseling, respite care (likely
  also nursing care, bereavement counseling, retreats)
- **Family support:** activities, childcare, meals, household help
  (cleaning/laundry) (likely also tutoring, camp, holidays and outings)
- **Tikva House:** links to the Tikva House page

This grouping is a candidate structure for the Services page cleanup (Phase 2
open item: current categories have duplicates, e.g. counseling ×2, retreats ×3,
tickets ×2, and odd groupings).

## Tikva House: section order (done for the prototype, 11/16/2026)

Primary audience on this page: **families** (unlike Home). Sections follow a
family's questions: is this for me, what's it like, how do I get a stay; donors
after.

1. **Hero** (families): intro, blurb, Request a stay as tap-to-call
2. **What's included**: inside the house (seven suites, amenities list)
3. **Request a Stay**: call, address, phone, email (moved here from Plan Your
   Stay). Ends with a one-line link to Plan Your Stay (wording TBD).
4. **Donate**: "Keep the lights on at Tikva House"; should land on Donate with
   Tikva House preselected as the fund
5. **Footer**

Plan Your Stay teaser section dropped (11/16/2026), folded into Request a Stay
as a link: it overlapped with What's included, there was little to say yet, and
the link lands when a family is thinking about logistics. Plan Your Stay covers
everything _around_ the stay (travel, parking, getting to the hospital,
check-in, what to bring, house rules), never the house's amenities.

- **Requests are phone-only (confirmed with client).** "Request a stay" =
  tap-to-call; no request form.
- Plan Your Stay link goes to a placeholder page in the prototype (its content
  is an open Phase 2 item).
- **Page list knock-on:** the approved Phase 1 list still has "Tikva House >
  Request a Stay: Form, with policies and guidelines." With phone-only requests
  that page has no job; its policies/guidelines could move to Plan Your Stay.
  Raise with the client before changing the approved list.

## Hero layout (11/16/2026)

## Images (11/16/2026)

Use images. First rule: privacy and dignity. Places are always fine; volunteers
and staff with permission; families served and children only with written
consent. Full image rules (placement, crop, treatment, alt text, quality bar)
come later, not for the prototype.

- **Tikva House:** 3 good interiors + 1 exterior. Hero = best interior; What's
  included = other two interiors; Request a Stay = exterior beside the address.
  Drop the 6 weaker interiors. No gallery page or lightbox. Ask client for a
  photo shoot of the house.
- **Home hero:** show the client two versions:
  - Woman in wheelchair cheering after the 5K (2560×1707): strongest, but she is
    identifiable (consent needed if JCN serves her) and 5K branding is prominent
    (5K is retired; crop tightly).
  - 5K shot (2560×1860), cropped to minimize 5K branding.
  - Fallback: brand-color panel.
- **Set aside:** kids making havdala (children, consent; portrait orientation).
- **Staff photos (46+):** not for the prototype; expect inconsistency on Board &
  Staff later.
